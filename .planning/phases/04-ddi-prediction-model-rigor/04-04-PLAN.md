Create `scripts/baselines_ddi.py`:
1. **Fingerprint Pair Representation**:
   - Compute 2048-bit radius-2 Morgan fingerprints for drug pairs: $a, b \in \{0, 1\}^{2048}$.
   - Combine symmetrically: $[a+b, a \odot b, |a-b|]$ (vector dimension: 6144).
2. **Logistic Regression Baseline**:
   - Train `sklearn.linear_model.LogisticRegression(max_iter=500, C=1.0)` on `data/splits/train_pairs.csv`.
3. **XGBoost Baseline**:
   - Train `xgboost.XGBClassifier(n_estimators=100, max_depth=6, learning_rate=0.1, n_jobs=-1)` on `data/splits/train_pairs.csv`.
4. **Nearest-Neighbor Tanimoto Baseline**:
   - Query pair $(A, B)$ evaluated against all training positives $(T_1, T_2)$:
     $$\text{score}(A, B) = \max_{(T_1, T_2) \in \mathcal{P}_{train}} \max\left( \frac{J(A, T_1) + J(B, T_2)}{2}, \frac{J(A, T_2) + J(B, T_1)}{2} \right)$$
5. Save models and provide evaluation hooks for test sets S0, S1, S2.
